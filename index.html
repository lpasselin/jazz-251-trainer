How to write exercises & patterns

Exercises box — one per line, weight 0–100 (0 = off, a 100 line is drawn 10× as often as a 10 line):

maj251 Eb 100pattern in a key (patterns defined in the box below)
chord Bb9 100single chord, any symbol
custom "my lick" "Dm9 G13 C6/9" 100fixed symbols shown verbatim; the name is the revealed answer

Patterns box — define your own: name Label = Roman chords. Degrees I…VII of the major scale with optional b/# (bII7, #Idim7); whatever follows the degree is printed as-is (IIm7b5 → IIm7♭5). Example: myturn My turnaround = IIIm7 VI7b9 IIm7 bII7 Imaj7, then weight it in the exercises box: myturn Bb 80.

Keys use #/b (C, F#, Bb…). Lines starting with # are comments. The preset pills below overwrite the exercises box — difficulty levels first, then famous players' favorite changes. Tap the card, press Space, or use ◀/▶ (or ←/→) to move; ◀ scrolls back through everything shown this session. auto-next is seconds per chord symbol — at 1.2, a 3-chord exercise advances after 3.6 s (0 = off). copy share link packs both boxes into a URL — opening it loads the lists (e.g. your teacher sends you the week's list).
